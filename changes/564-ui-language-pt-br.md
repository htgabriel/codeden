- **Interface in Brazilian Portuguese.** Settings › General › Appearance has a **Language** choice: Auto (the browser's language), English or Português (Brasil). The sidebar, settings navigation, inbox, orchestrator and PM chats, the composer, the thread header, the project page and the factory panel are translated, along with relative times, session statuses, permission levels and the session actions (archive, fork, handoff, review); the rest of the interface follows in later releases and shows English until then. Agents, prompts and the server's messages stay in English.
